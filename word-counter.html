Word and Character Counter

Count words, characters, lines, sentences and paragraphs as you type, with estimated reading and speaking time. Works for CJK text too.

How to use the Word Counter

  1. Paste your text into the box — every number updates as you type.
  2. Check Characters (no spaces) when you are fitting a strict limit such as a meta description or a tweet.
  3. Use the reading time estimate for blog posts and the speaking time estimate for scripts.

Frequently asked questions

How are Chinese and Japanese characters counted?

Each CJK character counts as one word, which is the convention most editors use. Latin text is split on whitespace, and the two counts are not double counted.

How is reading time calculated?

At 220 words per minute for silent reading and 130 for speaking out loud, both rounded to the nearest second.

Is my text stored?

No. The counter never leaves your browser and nothing is saved between visits.
