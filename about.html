About ToolBench

ToolBench is a small collection of everyday utilities — formatters, converters, counters — built as static pages with no backend. There is no account system, no upload endpoint and no database, because none of these tools need one.

How it works

Each page loads a few kilobytes of HTML, CSS and JavaScript. The logic that formats your JSON or hashes your text runs inside your browser using standard web APIs such as JSON.parse, TextEncoder and crypto.subtle. Nothing you paste is transmitted, so the tools keep working offline once loaded.

Why it exists

Most utility sites are slow, plastered with interstitials and quietly log the values people paste into them. A JWT or an API key in a random form field is a bad habit, and it is unnecessary: the compute here costs nothing, so it can happen on your side of the wire.

Keeping the lights on

The site is funded by advertising. Ads are served by third-party networks that may set cookies of their own; the privacy policy explains what that means and how to opt out. No advertiser sees what you type into a tool.

Corrections

Found a bug, a wrong conversion factor or a page that breaks on your browser? Write to you@example.com and it gets fixed.
