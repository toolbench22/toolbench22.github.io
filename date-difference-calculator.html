Date Difference Calculator

Count days between two dates, including business days and weekend days, plus weeks, months, years and hours. Swap the dates in one click.

How to use the Date Difference Calculator

  1. Enter the two dates. The order does not matter — use Swap to flip them.
  2. Read days, business days (Mon-Fri) and weekend days immediately.
  3. Weeks, months and years are averages, which is why they are labelled as such.

Frequently asked questions

What counts as a business day?

Monday to Friday, counted day by day between the two dates. Public holidays are not excluded, because they differ by country and are not knowable from the dates alone.

Why is "months" an average?

Months vary from 28 to 31 days. Dividing by 30.4375 gives a consistent figure for comparison; the exact calendar answer is in the days count.

Can the result be negative?

Yes, if the second date is earlier than the first. That is deliberate: the sign tells you direction, so you can use it in calculations without re-checking order.
