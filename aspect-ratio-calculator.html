Aspect Ratio Calculator

Reduce any width and height to its simplest aspect ratio, recognise common ratios like 16:9, and compute a proportional new width or height.

How to use the Aspect Ratio Calculator

  1. Enter the original width and height.
  2. Read the simplified ratio, the decimal value and the closest common name (16:9, 4:3, 21:9, 1:1 and others).
  3. Enter a new width or height to get the proportional counterpart, so images and videos never stretch.

Frequently asked questions

How is the ratio simplified?

Both numbers are divided by their greatest common divisor, so 1920x1080 becomes 16:9 exactly rather than an approximation.

Why does the new dimension round?

Pixel counts must be integers. The tool rounds to the nearest pixel, which is what image and video encoders require.

What is the difference between 16:9 and 16:10?

16:9 (1.78) is the widescreen video standard; 16:10 (1.6) is taller, common on laptops. Small decimal differences matter — the preset match only fires within 0.01.
