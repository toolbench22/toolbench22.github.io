URL Slug Generator

Turn titles into clean URL slugs: lowercase, accents stripped, spaces to hyphens, punctuation removed. Batch mode handles a whole list.

How to use the Slug Generator

  1. Paste one title per line to process a whole batch at once.
  2. Choose hyphen or underscore separators. Hyphens are the safer choice for SEO.
  3. Copy the result and use it as the post slug, file name or anchor id.

Frequently asked questions

Hyphens or underscores in URLs?

Hyphens. Search engines treat a hyphen as a word separator, while an underscore is often read as joining two words.

What happens to accented characters?

They are decomposed and the diacritics removed, so é becomes e and ü becomes u, which keeps slugs readable and avoids percent-encoding.

Should slugs be short?

Shorter is better for sharing and for reading in search results. Aim for three to five meaningful words and drop stop words like a, the, of.
