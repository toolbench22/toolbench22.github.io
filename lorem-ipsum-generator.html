Lorem Ipsum Placeholder Generator

Generate placeholder Latin text by the paragraph, sentence or word for mockups and layout tests. Choose the amount, copy in one click.

How to use the Lorem Ipsum Generator

  1. Choose the unit: paragraphs for page layouts, sentences for cards and callouts, words for labels.
  2. Set the amount, between 1 and 50.
  3. Press Generate, then Copy. The text is random each time so repeated blocks do not look identical.

Frequently asked questions

Why Latin and not real English?

A reader's eye ignores Latin but starts reading English, so reviewers comment on the copy instead of the layout. That is exactly what placeholder text is for.

Is the output always identical?

No. Words are drawn randomly on each run, which is more useful for realistic design work than repeating one fixed paragraph.

Can I get exactly 100 words?

Choose the words unit and set 100. Paragraph and sentence units aim for natural length instead of an exact word count.
