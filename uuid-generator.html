UUID v4 Generator

Generate up to 500 random RFC 4122 version 4 UUIDs at once using the browser crypto API. Uppercase, braces and copy-to-clipboard included.

How to use the UUID Generator

  1. Set how many UUIDs you need, from 1 to 500.
  2. Tick uppercase or braces if the system you are feeding expects that format.
  3. Press Generate, then Copy — the whole list goes to your clipboard as one UUID per line.

Frequently asked questions

Are these UUIDs random enough for production?

They come from crypto.getRandomValues, the browser's cryptographically secure generator, so they are suitable for identifiers and session keys. They are not sequential, so they are poor primary keys in some databases.

Can two generated UUIDs collide?

Version 4 UUIDs carry 122 random bits. You would need to generate billions per second for decades before a collision became likely.

Where is the list stored?

Nowhere. Nothing is sent to a server and nothing is persisted after you close the tab.
