Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-10-18

The short version

Tool input never leaves your device. We do not ask for an account, an email address or a name. There is no server-side code that could store your text, and no analytics product records the contents of a textarea.

What is processed locally

Everything you type, paste, upload into the image field or generate (passwords, UUIDs, hashes) is handled by JavaScript in your browser tab. Closing the tab discards it. We do not use localStorage to store tool input; the only stored value is a timestamp used to rate-limit a popunder to once per day.

Server logs

The site is served as static files by a hosting provider (Cloudflare Pages or similar). Like every web host, that provider sees the usual request metadata: IP address, user agent, requested path, timestamp. Those logs are used for abuse prevention and aggregate traffic counts. They do not contain what you typed into a tool.

Cookies and advertising

Advertising is delivered by third-party networks. Those networks and their partners may set cookies or read device identifiers to limit how often you see an ad and to measure performance. We do not control their cookies, and we do not share any tool input with them — they never receive it.

Typical third parties include ad networks and demand-side platforms; their own privacy policies govern their behaviour. If you are in the EEA, UK or Switzerland, you will be shown a consent prompt where required, and you can refuse personalised advertising.

Your choices

  • Block or clear cookies in your browser settings — the tools keep working, because they do not depend on cookies.
  • Use a browser or extension that blocks third-party requests. Some ad revenue is lost, functionality is not.
  • Opt out of personalised advertising through the industry pages run by your advertising self-regulatory body, or through your device's ad settings.
  • Write to you@example.com with any privacy question or request.

Children

The site is a general-purpose utility and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes

If this policy changes materially, the date at the top changes with it.
