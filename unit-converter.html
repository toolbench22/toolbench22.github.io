Unit Converter (Length, Weight, Temperature, Data, Speed, Area, Volume)

Convert length, mass, temperature, digital storage, time, speed, area and volume with precise SI-based factors and a one-click swap.

How to use the Unit Converter

  1. Pick the category — length, mass, temperature, storage, time, speed, area or volume.
  2. Type the value and choose the from and to units. The result updates as you type.
  3. Use the swap arrow to reverse the direction without re-entering anything.

Frequently asked questions

Are the conversion factors exact?

SI-based units use exact definitions (1 inch = 0.0254 m exactly, 1 lb = 0.45359237 kg exactly). Storage uses both decimal (KB = 1000 B) and binary (KiB = 1024 B) units because both are in real use.

Why is a 1 TB drive only 931 GiB in my OS?

Drive makers use decimal TB while some operating systems display binary TiB but label it TB. Pick TiB in the converter to see the number your file manager shows.

How precise is the output?

Results are rounded to 12 significant digits, which removes floating point noise such as 0.30000000000000004 without losing anything you would type by hand. Values outside 1e-6 to 1e12 switch to exponential notation.
