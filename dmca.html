DMCA and Copyright

All tool code and explanatory text on this site is original. If you believe something here infringes your copyright, send a notice to you@example.com containing the elements below.

A valid notice includes

  1. Identification of the copyrighted work you claim is infringed.
  2. The exact URL on this site where the allegedly infringing material appears.
  3. Your name, address, telephone number and email address.
  4. A statement that you have a good-faith belief the use is not authorised by the copyright owner, its agent or the law.
  5. A statement, under penalty of perjury, that the information in the notice is accurate and that you are the owner or authorised to act on the owner's behalf.
  6. Your physical or electronic signature.

Valid notices are acted on promptly, usually within a few business days. Counter-notices are handled the same way.

Note on third-party content

This site hosts no user-uploaded content and no third-party media library, so the usual DMCA surface does not exist here. Advertising creatives are supplied by ad networks and are reported to those networks rather than removed from this site directly.
