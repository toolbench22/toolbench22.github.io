HEX to RGB and HSL Color Converter

Convert between HEX, RGB and HSL, see relative luminance, and find out which text color stays readable on top of the color you picked.

How to use the Color Converter

  1. Type or paste a hex color — 3, 6 or 8 digits all work.
  2. Read HEX, RGB, HSL and relative luminance instantly, plus the text color that stays legible on it.
  3. Press "Pick random" when you need a starting point for a palette.

Frequently asked questions

What is relative luminance used for?

It estimates perceived brightness. The number is the input to the WCAG contrast ratio formula, so it tells you whether black or white text will be readable.

How do I read HSL?

Hue in degrees (0-360), saturation and lightness in percent. HSL is handy for building hover and active states: keep H and S, shift L.

Does it support transparency?

8-digit hex is accepted and the alpha is ignored, because RGB and HSL output here are opaque. Reduce the channel values if you need a blended color.
