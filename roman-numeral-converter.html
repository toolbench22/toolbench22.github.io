Roman Numeral Converter

Convert numbers 1-3999 to Roman numerals and decode Roman numerals back to numbers, with validation of non-standard forms like IIII.

How to use the Roman Numeral Converter

  1. Type a number (1-3999) or a Roman numeral into the box — it detects which you meant.
  2. Numbers convert to numerals; anything made of I V X L C D M converts to a number.
  3. Invalid forms such as IIII or VX are rejected with the canonical spelling suggested.

Frequently asked questions

Why does it stop at 3999?

Classical Roman numerals have no standard symbol above M (1000). Numbers beyond 3999 need overlines or non-standard extensions, so they are rejected rather than guessed.

Why is IIII invalid?

Subtractive notation is standard: 4 is IV, not IIII. Clock faces sometimes use IIII for symmetry, but that is a typographic convention, not a numeral rule.

Is MCMXCIV correct?

Yes — M (1000) + CM (900) + XC (90) + IV (4) = 1994.
