Contact

Email: you@example.com

What to include

  • Bug reports — the tool name, your browser and version, and the exact input that produced the wrong output.
  • Advertising — a screenshot of the ad and the page URL. Abusive or deceptive creatives are removed from the rotation as soon as they are confirmed.
  • Privacy requests — say what you are asking about; since no accounts or tool input are stored, most requests resolve with an explanation rather than a data export.
  • Copyright — see the DMCA page for the required format.

Replies usually take a few business days.
