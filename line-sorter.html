Line Sorter, Deduplicator and Shuffler

Sort lines A-Z, numerically, by length or randomly; remove duplicates, drop empty lines and trim spaces. Great for cleaning keyword and ID lists.

How to use the Line Sorter

  1. Paste your list, one item per line.
  2. Pick a sort mode: A to Z, numeric, by length or shuffle. Tick remove duplicates and drop empty lines to clean the list.
  3. The result appears on the right and updates live as you change options.

Frequently asked questions

Will deduping keep the first or last occurrence?

The first. Order is preserved except for the duplicates that were dropped, so a stable sort still behaves predictably.

What does the numeric sort do?

It parses the leading number of each line, so 2 comes before 10 — unlike a plain string sort, which would put 10 first.

Is the shuffle random?

It uses crypto-random keys per line, which is a fair shuffle for playlists, test samples and giveaway draws.
