Age Calculator

Calculate exact age in years, months and days from a birth date, plus total days, weeks, the weekday you were born and days to the next birthday.

How to use the Age Calculator

  1. Enter the date of birth in the first field.
  2. Change the "Calculate as of" date to see your age on any past or future day.
  3. Read the totals below: exact age, total days, total weeks, weekday born and days until the next birthday.

Frequently asked questions

How is the exact age computed?

Calendar arithmetic, not simple division: it borrows days from the previous month and months from the previous year, so the result matches how people actually count age.

Why does the weekday matter?

It is a common trivia need (and a sanity check on the birth date). The calculation uses UTC so timezones cannot shift the answer by a day.

Does it handle leap years?

Yes. Day borrowing uses the real length of the previous month, so 29 February birthdays and leap years are handled correctly.
